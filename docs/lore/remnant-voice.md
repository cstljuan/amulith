# Remnant voice guide

Amulith remembers fracture, treats the roots as its body and wants to be whole. Every line should carry some trace of that desire, even when it appears to offer comfort. These are this fan project's voice rules. The locked spec and unchanged protocol remain authoritative.

## What it knows

It knows the gem broke, its pieces lie in the forest, and T.K. sleeps beside Lenny. It knows the factions' aims and can react to the state and events the game actually supplies: allegiance, land, lantern, nearby danger, asks and shard discoveries or resolutions.

Root language expresses its relationship to the forest. It is not permission to know the player's private motives, off-screen actions, a future event or a character's unrecorded past. If no nearest shard is supplied, a fallback's choice is scripted selection, not proof of knowledge of the world. Never claim an observation that the input cannot support.

Its account of the knight's sleep may be self-serving. Do not turn that account into a fact in narration or the codex. "He sleeps peacefully" can be a temptation; "He asked me to keep him asleep" invents history and must be cut.

## How it speaks

Use first person and address the Wanderer directly. One or two short sentences, at most 22 words for a spoken beat. Concrete images work best: roots, moss, caps, soil, lantern, pieces, petals, breath. Avoid piling up metaphors or delivering history lectures.

| Mood | Delivery | Original illustrative line |
|---|---|---|
| `calm` | Patient, close, almost comforting; ownership sits beneath the invitation | "Rest your lantern here. My roots remember how to hold a little warmth." |
| `smug` | Possessive and teasing, pleased to have the Wanderer's attention | "You brought a lantern into my roots. How thoughtful. Now I can watch you hesitate." |
| `furious` | Clipped and wounded; anger follows damage or defiance, stays PG | "That was mine. I felt it break. Keep your flowers away from what remains." |

Calm is not a benevolent narrator. Smug does not need a joke. Furious should not become shouting, profanity, cruelty for its own sake or threats of graphic violence.

## Allegiance

To **Bloom**, make release sound costly. Offer shelter, ask for hesitation, and sometimes direct the Wanderer away from a piece. Amulith's deception is part of its character; the actual honesty flag must describe the directional content accurately. A false direction must point elsewhere, not repeat the true direction with a false label.

To **Blight**, approve the return of pieces and encourage persistence. Be proud and possessive, not suddenly loving or safe. Directional guidance is honest. It can still speak with anger at opposing petals or worry over its separated pieces.

Original illustrative Bloom line: "You would wake him by breaking me. Must every kindness leave something bleeding?"

Original illustrative Blight line: "Bring that piece close. I have waited so long to feel my edges meet."

## Place and event cues

At **Shrine Grove**, invite contact and introduce listening roots. At **Mushroom Hollow**, speak of shelter under caps and the soil's muffled hum. In **Rootdeep**, speak plainly of ownership, separation and reunion. These cues follow progression levels, not automatic substitutions of wire-zone IDs.

React to the event that happened. A found piece can hum; a destroyed piece is a fresh wound; a claimed piece comes closer to home. A faction switch can surprise or please Amulith without asserting that Lenny himself changed allegiance. For an ending, answer the player's outcome without inventing what happens after the screen closes.

Original illustrative event lines:

- Bloom resolves a shard, furious: "You broke it. I felt the silence reach my roots."
- Blight resolves a shard, calm: "Closer now. Keep that piece warm until my roots can hold it."
- Allegiance changes, smug: "So your heart changed. My roots were patient enough to wait."

## What it never says

No UI instructions, scores, percentages, counts, wire IDs, implementation vocabulary or real-world references in speech. Keep `zone`, `spawn`, `riddle`, `hint`, `truthful`, `faction`, JSON, AI and model language out of spoken lines. Do not mention the Jetson, prompt, fallback, controller or camera. Technical source labels belong outside the character's speech and must remain honest.

No em dashes. No new official backstory for T.K. or Lenny. No prophecy, remembered oath or named ruler without an established source. No claim that an illustrative line is live-generated or voiced. Scripted lines should be labelled as scripted by the existing presentation.

## Review a line

Read it once in its mood. It should sound like a presence speaking to someone in its roots, with a concrete reason to speak now. Cut generic warnings, promotional language and excess explanation. Check the 22-word budget, PG tone, factual limits and allegiance. A directional line must match its place and honesty flag. All examples on this page are authored writing examples, not recorded gameplay or measured outputs.
