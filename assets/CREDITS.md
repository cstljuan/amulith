# Amulith landing page assets

Lenny, T.K., the gem, and the Knight Hacks IX lore belong to the Knight Hacks design team: Lena Tran, Gabriela Zambrano, Christina Nguyen, David Navarrete, and Amira Bhuiyan. This is a non-commercial fan project made at Knight Hacks IX.

## Game art

Existing game art directed by Juan Castillo with AI-assisted generation through Higgsfield. The game's art credits name GPT Image 2.5 and the generation date 2026-10-11. No new art was generated for this page.

- `art/shrine-keyart.jpg` is a byte-for-byte copy of `game/docs/keyart/keyart-a.jpg`. It is concept art, not a gameplay screenshot.
- `art/wanderer-bloom.png` and `art/wanderer-blight.png` are the first idle frame from the corresponding `game/public/art/wanderer/sheet-*.png`. Transparent padding was cropped, then each frame was reduced by a factor of two with nearest-neighbor sampling.
- `art/amulith-gem.png` comes from `game/public/art/props/amulith-gem.png`, reduced by a factor of two with nearest-neighbor sampling.
- Pixel sprites render at their native derived dimensions (1x), using `image-rendering: pixelated`.
- `favicon.svg` is a simple code-drawn gem mark for this site.
